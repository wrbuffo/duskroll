Common questions

How do I restore Duskroll Pro on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in Duskroll (the ⋯ menu on the camera) and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought Pro with. Pro is a one-time purchase; there's no subscription.

How do I edit a photo from Apple Photos?

Open the photo in Photos, tap Edit, then the ⋯ button and choose Duskroll. If it isn't listed, tap More in that menu and switch Duskroll on.

Where are my photos stored?

Inside Duskroll on your iPhone. Turn on "Save camera shots to Photos" in Settings to also keep a copy in your photo library. Deleting the app deletes the photos stored in it.

How do I undo or start over on an edit?

Tap Undo on the photo to step back. To start over, use Reset to original in the editor's ⋯ menu. Long-press a tab (Film, Optics, Adjust, Darkroom) to reset just that tab.

What's a roll?

Load a roll to shoot 12–72 frames on one film without seeing them until they're "developed", like real film. Tap Roll on the camera to start one.
