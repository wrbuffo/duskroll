Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

In short: Duskroll doesn't collect your data. Your photos are processed and stored on your iPhone and are never uploaded. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Duskroll is an iPhone app published by an independent developer, referred to here as "we". You can reach us at duskroll.app@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. Duskroll has no server and does not send your photos, edits, location or any other personal information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Photos and edits. Photos you take or import, and your edits, rolls and settings, are stored inside the app on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Camera. Used only to show the viewfinder and take pictures.
  • Photo library. Duskroll only adds photos you choose to save. Photos you import are picked by you with Apple's photo picker; Duskroll can't browse your library.
  • Location (optional, off by default). If you turn on "Tag photos with location", the location is saved with your photos on your device and in photos you export. You can remove it from exports in Settings.
  • Photo processing. Film looks, lens effects and subject detection all run on your iPhone using Apple's frameworks. Nothing is sent to the cloud.
  • Edit with Duskroll in Photos. The editing extension works inside Apple's Photos app and saves your edit there, on your device or in your own iCloud Photos.

Purchases

Duskroll Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, card or Apple Account details. Apple tells the app only whether Pro has been purchased.

Sharing

Photos leave your device only when you choose to share or save them, using the iOS share sheet or Save to Photos.

Crash reports

If you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash and performance reports with us. These don't include your photos. You can turn this off in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Duskroll doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions? Email duskroll.app@gmail.com.
